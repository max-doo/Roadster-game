# Roadster: Gravity Breaker
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Delegated by the user's explicit Owner mandate. Vite, TypeScript, React, Three.js, Rapier 2D, Web Audio and localStorage. Static deployment, no accounts or backend.
## Users
Players using landscape mobile browsers and desktop keyboard controls, seeking short, replayable physics stunt challenges.
## Product Purpose
Deliver three complete levels centered on acceleration, underside adhesion, finite cold-gas thrust and pitch control. PLAN.md is the acceptance authority.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Fixed-step planar rigid-body physics synchronized with a full 3D original car. Clear failure, checkpoints, retry, progression, stars and best records. Multi-touch controls. No unverified claims of real-device performance or official vehicle fidelity.
## Brand Commitments
Original independent game identity; aerospace automotive and cinematic industrial futurism. Warm white hero vehicle, graphite and titanium structures, cyan adhesion and orange thrust. No Tesla logo or claim of affiliation. The October 2026 vehicle is the reference, not the 2017 concept.
## Evidence on Hand
PLAN.md and linked public reference pages. No supplied licensed vehicle model. Original assets must record their provenance and uncertain details.
## Product Principles
Playable first, beautiful always. Real force-based mechanics. Mobile-first input. Actual visual verification. No fake completion.
